What is free

Painting tools, color palettes, illustrated face guides, saving and editing paintings, live face tracking on compatible devices, and branded video export are free. The eight First Doodles stickers are included. Popmask has no ads and no subscriptions.

Optional sticker packs

Additional sticker collections are optional, permanent non-consumable purchases. Each purchase unlocks the named collection; it is not a recurring charge. Apple displays the local price and purchase terms before confirmation and processes payment. Purchases require an Apple Account and an internet connection. A parent or guardian should manage purchases for children.

Use Restore purchases in Settings with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoration and downloading pack artwork require a connection. A permanent unlock means no recurring fee for that pack; it does not promise that every device, OS version or service will remain supported indefinitely. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, subject to Apple’s terms and applicable law. A refunded purchase can lose its unlock.

Device support and saved work

Popmask requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. The live mirror requires a device supporting ARKit face tracking and camera permission. Microphone permission is optional for recording sound. The painting studio remains available when face tracking is unsupported.

Paintings are stored locally. Keep backups of work you want to retain. Removing the app removes its local sketchbook; exported copies and device backups are separate. Tracking quality depends on the device, lighting, visibility and movement. Software updates may change features or compatibility.
