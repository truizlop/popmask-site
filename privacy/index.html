Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Your face belongs to you. Your paintings stay in your sketchbook.

Popmask is developed by Tomás Ruiz López. This policy covers the Popmask app and this website. For questions or privacy requests, contact truizlop@gmail.com.

Camera and face tracking

With your permission, Popmask uses the camera and Apple’s ARKit face-tracking information to align your painting with your face in the live mirror. Camera frames and face geometry are processed on your device. We do not use them to identify you, build a biometric profile, save facial geometry, or transmit camera frames or facial geometry to us, RevenueCat, advertisers or another server.

The illustrated face guides are app artwork. Saved paintings contain your drawing, sticker placements and the chosen illustrated guide, not a scan of your face.

Recordings and permissions

Recording creates a video on your device. The microphone is used only with permission when you choose to include sound. Photos access is requested when you choose to save a finished video. Saving or sharing a recording is your choice. Copies you save to Photos may sync through your own iCloud settings; a destination you select in the share sheet handles the shared file under its own policy.

You can manage Camera, Microphone and Photos permissions in your device’s Settings. Painting does not require camera or microphone access. Temporary recording files may remain in the app’s local storage until cleaned up or the app is removed; copies you save elsewhere remain under your control.

Your local sketchbook

Paintings, previews, preferences and sticker placements are stored locally. Your device backups may include them. Delete a painting in the sketchbook to remove its local file. Removing the app removes its local sketchbook; it does not remove exported copies or existing backups. Popmask does not operate a cloud artwork account.

Optional purchases

Apple processes sticker-pack payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. Popmask uses RevenueCat to retrieve products, validate transactions, restore purchases and determine which permanent sticker packs you own.

RevenueCat processes an automatically generated anonymous app-user identifier, purchase and transaction history, product identifiers, ownership status, and app/device information needed to provide the purchase service, such as app and OS versions and storefront locale or currency. We do not provide your name, email, a custom identifiable account ID, an advertising identifier, your paintings or your face data.

Purchase information is used for app functionality, purchase restoration, fraud prevention, support, and purchase analytics in RevenueCat’s dashboard. It is not used by Popmask for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

No ads or behavioral tracking

Popmask has no advertising and no separate usage-analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information. Apple may provide developers with diagnostics or aggregate App Store reports according to your device and Apple Account settings.

Support and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Please do not send face recordings unless you choose to and they are necessary for your request.

Purchase records are retained as needed to verify permanent ownership, restore access, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Deleting the app does not erase records held by Apple or RevenueCat and does not refund a purchase. We cannot retrieve or delete artwork that exists only on your device.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of information we can associate with you, or request a portable copy. Contact us to exercise these rights or raise a concern. We may need limited transaction information to locate an anonymous purchase record; never send your Apple Account password. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where applicable, purchase processing supports the performance of your purchase and our legitimate interests in reliable service and fraud prevention; support communications serve our legitimate interest in responding to you. Camera and microphone access depends on your permission. Providers may process information in other countries under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

Children and families

Popmask does not ask for a name, age or account. Apple’s purchase controls apply to optional sticker packs. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent through support. Please ask permission before recording or sharing someone else’s face.

This website

This site has no advertising, added analytics scripts, tracking pixels or embedded third-party media. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. GitHub Pages may process ordinary connection and security information, including IP addresses, to serve and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External links have their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the app.
